# Credit and debit card dispute policy

## Debit card disputes

- Unauthorized debit card transactions are governed by Regulation E; follow the error resolution policy and its provisional credit timelines.
- Disputes with merchants where the charge was authorized (wrong item, service not delivered) require the member to contact the merchant first, then file a Visa/Mastercard dispute.

## Credit card disputes

- Billing errors on credit cards are governed by Regulation Z and the Fair Credit Billing Act.
- Members have 60 days from the statement date to dispute a billing error in writing.
- The card issuer must acknowledge the dispute within 30 days and resolve it within two complete billing cycles, not more than 90 days.
- The member may withhold payment on the disputed amount during the investigation, and no interest may accrue on it.

## Common dispute categories

- Duplicate charge: refund through the merchant first, dispute second.
- Cancelled recurring charge: member must cancel with the merchant; keep evidence of the cancellation.
- Credit not processed: a merchant refund that never posted, dispute after 15 days.
- Card-not-present fraud: reissue the card immediately and confirm the shipping address.

## Agent checklist

- Verify the last four digits and confirm whether the physical card is still in the member's possession.
- For fraud, reissue first and dispute second.
- Give the member the provisional credit date in writing where Regulation E applies.
