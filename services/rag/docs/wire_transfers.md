# Domestic and international wire transfer policy

## Cut-offs and timing

- Domestic wires submitted before 4:00 PM ET are sent the same business day; funds typically arrive same day.
- International wires submitted before 2:00 PM ET are sent the same business day; arrival is typically 1 to 5 business days depending on the destination country and intermediary banks.

## Fees

- Outgoing domestic wire: $25. Incoming domestic wire: $0 for consumer accounts.
- Outgoing international wire: $45 plus any intermediary bank fees.
- Intermediary banks along the payment route may deduct lifting fees, so the beneficiary can receive less than the amount sent. This must be disclosed in the wire agreement before the member signs.

## Consumer international remittance rights (Regulation E subpart B)

- For consumer international wires, the member may cancel within 30 minutes of payment for a full refund, provided the funds have not been picked up or deposited.
- The member may dispute an error within 180 days of the disclosed date of availability.
- The fee, exchange rate, and amount to be received must be disclosed before payment.

## Recalls and traces

- A recall can be requested for wires sent in error, but the receiving bank is not obligated to return funds once credited to the beneficiary.
- Wire traces for missing wires are initiated after 3 business days for domestic and 7 business days for international.
- Trace requests are free for members when the sending error was ours.

## Fraud prevention

- Branch staff must complete a verbal warning checklist for any member sending a wire to a first-time beneficiary above $10,000, covering common romance, real estate, and grandparent scam patterns.
