# Regulation E error resolution policy

Applies to consumer accounts at Sample Federal Credit Union. Follows 12 CFR 1005.11.

## What counts as an error

- Unauthorized electronic fund transfers (debit card, ACH, ATM withdrawals)
- Incorrect EFT amounts posted to the member's account
- Missing funds from a deposit or transfer
- Statement errors involving electronic transactions

## Member notice requirements

- Members must report errors within 60 days of the statement date on which the error first appeared.
- After that window, the credit union may still assist, but Regulation E liability limits no longer apply.

## Investigation timeline

- Acknowledge the claim and begin investigation within 10 business days.
- If the investigation cannot be completed in 10 business days, provisionally credit the member's account and extend the investigation up to 45 days (90 days for new accounts, point-of-sale, or foreign transactions).
- Report the outcome to the member within 3 business days of completing the investigation.

## Member liability limits

- $0 if the card was lost or stolen and reported before any unauthorized use.
- Up to $50 if reported within 2 business days of learning of the loss.
- Up to $500 if reported after 2 business days but within 60 days of the statement.

## Agent guidance

- Always restate the timeline back to the member in writing.
- Never promise a specific outcome before the investigation concludes.
- Provisionally credit first, investigate second, when the 10-day window will be exceeded.
