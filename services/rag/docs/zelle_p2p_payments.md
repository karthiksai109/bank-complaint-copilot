# Zelle and person-to-person payment policy

## Irreversibility

- Zelle payments to enrolled recipients are typically completed within minutes and cannot be cancelled or reversed once the recipient is enrolled.
- If the recipient is not yet enrolled, the sender can cancel the payment from the app before the recipient enrolls.

## Authorized scam payments

- Payments the member personally authorized, even if induced by a scammer, are generally not covered under Regulation E.
- We still file a claim with the recipient's bank and, where the recipient account is known to be used for fraud, request a recall in good faith. Outcome is not guaranteed.
- Coach members: the credit union will never call and ask them to send money to themselves, and will never ask for a one-time passcode.

## Unauthorized Zelle payments

- If the member did not authorize the payment at all (account takeover, stolen credentials), treat it as a Regulation E error and follow the error resolution policy, including provisional credit timelines.

## Claims workflow

1. Confirm date, amount, recipient identifier (phone or email), and device used.
2. Freeze further outbound Zelle activity if takeover is suspected.
3. File the network claim within 24 hours and document the case ID for the member.
4. Set expectations: scam claims often take 30 to 45 days for a network decision.

## Escalation triggers

- Any claim above $3,000.
- Repeated claims from the same member within 6 months, which may need fraud prevention review.
- Suspected elder financial exploitation, which must be reported under the Senior Safe Act escalation path.
