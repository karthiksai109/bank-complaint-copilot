# Mortgage servicing policy

Follows RESPA (Regulation X) provisions on mortgage servicing. Applies to all mortgages serviced in-house at Sample Federal Credit Union.

## Payment application

- Payments are applied first to current interest, then principal, then escrow, then fees.
- Extra principal payments must be applied to principal when the member clearly designates them as such. If the intent is ambiguous, apply per the note and confirm intent in writing before reapplying.
- Payments received by 3:00 PM CT are credited the same business day.

## Escrow accounts

- Escrow analyses run annually. Changes to the monthly escrow amount require a notice at least 30 days before the new amount takes effect.
- Escrow shortages may be collected in equal monthly installments over at least 12 months; lump-sum collection requires member consent.

## Private mortgage insurance (PMI)

- Members may request PMI cancellation at 80% loan-to-value of the original value, subject to good payment history and no subordinate liens.
- PMI terminates automatically at 78% LTV of the original amortization schedule for members current on payments.
- LTV disputes require a new appraisal at member expense unless the LTV is clearly met under the original schedule.

## Delinquency and loss mitigation

- Members become loss-mitigation eligible at 45 days delinquent; outreach must begin by day 36.
- A complete loss mitigation application must be reviewed within 30 days of receipt.
- Dual tracking is prohibited: we may not make first notice or move to judgment while a timely, complete loss mitigation application is under review. This is the single most litigated servicing rule — never guess on timelines, quote them from the file.

## Payoff statements

- Payoff statements must be provided within 7 business days of a written request. The quoted figure includes per-diem interest through the stated good-through date.

## Force-placed insurance

- Hazard or flood insurance may only be force-placed after a 45-day notice sequence (initial notice at day 45, reminder at day 30 before placement) proving the member's own coverage has lapsed.
- Force-placed charges must be removed within 15 days of the member providing evidence of continuous coverage, and premiums for any overlap period refunded.
