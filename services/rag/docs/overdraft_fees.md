# Overdraft and NSF fee policy

## Fee schedule

- Overdraft fee: $32 per paid item, capped at 3 fees per business day.
- Non-sufficient funds (NSF) fee: $0. Returned items are declined without a fee. This was changed in 2023 and some members still reference the old $29 NSF fee.

## Opt-in rules for ATM and one-time debit transactions

- Under Regulation E section 1005.17, we may not charge overdraft fees on ATM or one-time debit card transactions unless the member has affirmatively opted in.
- Check the member's opt-in status in the core system before quoting any fee on these transactions.
- Members may revoke opt-in at any time, effective immediately for future transactions.

## Courtesy reversals

- Agents may reverse one overdraft fee per rolling 12 months as a courtesy, without supervisor approval.
- Two or more reversals in 12 months require team lead approval.
- Reversals issued for a bank error are not counted against the courtesy allowance.

## Transaction posting order

- Deposits post before withdrawals each business day.
- Debits post in chronological order where timestamps exist; otherwise low-to-high amounts.
- We do not reorder transactions high-to-low. If a member alleges reordering, pull the posting log and walk them through the order before escalating.

## Goodwill messaging

- Acknowledge the frustration, explain the timeline of the fee, then offer the courtesy reversal if eligible.
- Do not promise reversals you cannot approve.
